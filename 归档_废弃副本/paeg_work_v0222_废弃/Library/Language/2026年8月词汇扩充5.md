### 841. perspicacious
**音标**: /ˌpɜːspɪˈkeɪʃəs/
**词性**: adj.
**中文释义**: 敏锐的；具有洞察力的；聪明的
**英文释义**: having a ready insight; mentally perceptive; shrewd
**词根**: Latin *perspicax* = *per-* (through) + *specere* (to look)
**例句**: A perspicacious reader can deduce the author's true intentions from subtle clues in the text.
