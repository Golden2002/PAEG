### 211. current
**音标**: /ˈkʌrənt/
**词性**: adj./n.
**中文释义**: 当前的；水流，电流，趋势
**英文释义**: belonging to the present time / a flow of water or electricity / a tendency
**词根**: Latin *currere* (to run) + *-ent*
**一词多义**:
- The current situation is concerning. (当前的局势令人担忧。)
- The river has a strong current. (这条河水流湍急。)
- Fashion trends reflect the current of the times. (时尚潮流反映时代趋势。)
