### 421. scrutinize
**音标**: /ˈskruːtɪnaɪz/
**词性**: v.
**中文释义**: 仔细检查；详细审查
**英文释义**: to examine in detail / to inspect thoroughly
**词根**: Latin *scrutinium*
**例句**:
- The auditor scrutinized the accounts. (审计员仔细检查了账目。)
