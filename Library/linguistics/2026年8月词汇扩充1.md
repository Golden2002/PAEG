## Day 1

### 1. academic
**音标**：/ˌækəˈdemɪk/
**词性**：adj./n.
**中文释义**：学术的；大学生，学究
**英文释义**：relating to education and scholarship; a student or teacher at a college/university
**词根**：Greek *akademeia* (Plato's school) — from the garden of Akademos
**例句**：The **academic** performance of students improved significantly after the new teaching method was introduced.

---

### 2. access
**音标**：/ˈækses/
**词性**：n./v.
**中文释义**：进入权，使用权；访问，进入
**英文释义**：the means or opportunity to approach or enter; to reach or obtain
**词根**：Latin *accedere* (to approach) = *ad-* (to) + *cedere* (to go)
**例句**：Students need **access** to图书馆 resources to complete their research assignments.

---

### 3. achieve
**音标**：/əˈtʃiːv/
**词性**：v.
**中文释义**：达到，实现，获得
**英文释义**：to successfully reach a goal through effort; to accomplish
**词根**：Latin *ad* (to) + *caput* (head) — originally "to bring to a head"
**例句**：Through hard work and determination, she was able to **achieve** her dream of studying abroad.

---

### 4. acquire
**音标**：/əˈkwaɪə/
**词性**：v.
**中文释义**：获得，学到，收购
**英文释义**：to gain possession of something; to learn or develop a skill
**词根**：Latin *acquirere* = *ad-* (to) + *quirere* (to seek)
**例句**：The company plans to **acquire** its competitor to expand market share.

---

### 5. adequate
**音标**：/ˈædɪkwət/
**词性**：adj.
**中文释义**：足够的，适当的，勉强够的
**英文释义**：sufficient; enough for a particular purpose; satisfactory
**词根**：Latin *adaequare* (to make equal) = *ad-* (to) + *aequus* (equal)
**例句**：The hotel provided **adequate** facilities for a comfortable stay.

---

**中文释义**：调整，调节，适应

**词根**：Latin *ad-* (to) + *justus* (just, proper) — to put in proper order
**例句**：It took her a few weeks to **adjust** to the new schedule after moving to a different time zone.

---
