## Day 1

### 1. ubiquitous
**音标**：/juːˈbɪkwɪtəs/
**词性**：adj.
**中文释义**：无处不在的，普遍存在的
**英文释义**：present, appearing, or found everywhere
**词根**：Latin *ubique* (everywhere) = *ubi* (where) + *-que* (and/also)
**例句**：Smartphones have become **ubiquitous** in modern society, fundamentally changing how we communicate and access information.

---

### 2. ephemeral
**音标**：/ɪˈfemərəl/
**词性**：adj.
**中文释义**：短暂的，转瞬即逝的
**英文释义**：lasting for a very short time; transient
**词根**：Greek *ephemeros* (lasting only a day) = *epi-* (on) + *hemera* (day)
**例句**：The **ephemeral** nature of social media trends makes it challenging for brands to maintain lasting relevance.

---

### 3. pragmatic
**音标**：/præɡˈmætɪk/
**词性**：adj.
**中文释义**：务实的，实用主义的
**英文释义**：dealing with things sensibly and realistically; practical
**词根**：Greek *pragmatikos* (fit for business) from *pragma* (deed, act)
**例句**：The CEO adopted a **pragmatic** approach to the crisis, prioritizing immediate solutions over perfect long-term strategies.

---

### 4. ameliorate
**音标**：/əˈmiːliəreɪt/
**词性**：v.
**中文释义**：改善，改进，使变好
**英文释义**：to make something bad or unsatisfactory better
**词根**：Latin *ameliorare* = *melior* (better) + *ad-* (to)
**例句**：The new policies were designed to **ameliorate** the living conditions in urban slums and reduce poverty.

---

### 5. cacophony
**音标**：/kəˈkɒfəni/
**词性**：n.
**中文释义**：刺耳的声音，不和谐音
**英文释义**：a harsh, discordant mixture of sounds
**词根**：Greek *kakophonia* = *kakos* (bad) + *phone* (sound)
**例句**：The **cacophony** of car horns, construction, and shouting vendors filled the busy street market.

---

### 6. sycophant
**音标**：/ˈsɪkəfænt/
**词性**：n.
**中文释义**：谄媚者，马屁精，阿谀奉承者
**英文释义**：a person who acts obsequiously toward someone important to gain advantage
**词根**：Greek *sykophantes* originally meaning "one who shows the fig" — likely referring to an ancient obscene gesture; later associated with flatterers
**例句**：The dictator surrounded himself with **sycophants** who never dared to challenge his decisions, no matter how disastrous.

---

### 7. sycophantic
**音标**：/ˌsɪkəˈfæntɪk/
**词性**：adj.
**中文释义**：谄媚的，阿谀奉承的
**英文释义**：characterized by excessive flattery or servile behavior
**词根**：From *sycophant* + *-ic* (forming adjectives)
**例句**：His **sycophantic** behavior at the office—always agreeing with superiors and taking credit for others' work—earned him a poor reputation among colleagues.

---

### 12. laconic

**例句**：When asked for an explanation, he offered only a **laconic** response: "Mistakes were made."

### 18. vitiate

**例句**：The contract was **vitiated** by the discovery that one party had concealed a major financial liability.

---

### 19. esoteric

**英文释义**：intended for or understood by only a small number of people with specialized knowledge
